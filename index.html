Principal's Message

A Word from Our Principal

Watching our students take on the roles of diplomats, negotiators, and changemakers fills me with real hope for the future. MUN is far more than an academic exercise — it's a place where young people engage with the world's hardest problems, practise empathy, and learn the art of respectful dialogue. To every delegate who researches tirelessly, debates passionately, and listens deeply: you are already shaping the world. May this experience spark a lifelong passion for leadership, justice, and peace.
— Mr. Kollath Gopinath Menon
Principal, New Indian School Bahrain

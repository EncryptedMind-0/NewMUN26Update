Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend NewMUN 2026?
NewMUN is open to all high school and university students in Bahrain. You don't need prior MUN experience — just enthusiasm and a desire to learn!
What is the dress code?
Western business formal (suits, blazers, formal dresses) is required for all committee sessions throughout the conference.
How do I register my school?
School coordinators can register multiple delegates through our group registration form. Contact us at newmun@nisbh.com for school packages.
Will food be provided?
Yes! Lunch and refreshments are included for all registered delegates on both conference days. Vegetarian and dietary options are available.
What's the last date to register?
Regular registration closes on 20 September 2026. Early bird discounts are available until 31 August 2026.
Will there be awards?
Yes! Awards include Best Delegate, Outstanding Delegate, Honorable Mention, and Best Position Paper for each committee.
Can I participate if I've never done MUN before?
Absolutely! NewMUN is beginner-friendly. We provide training sessions and resources to help first-time delegates prepare.

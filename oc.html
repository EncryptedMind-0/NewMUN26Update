Aarav Kumar

Secretary-General

Aarav is a Grade 12 student with 4 years of MUN experience. He has chaired UNSC at NewMUN 2025 and was Best Delegate at BMUNIS 2025.

Sara Nasser

Deputy Secretary-General

Sara is a passionate debater and former Head of Media for NewMUN 2025. She specializes in international law and human rights advocacy.

Mohammed Al-Ali

Director of Committees

Mohammed has chaired 5 MUN conferences across Bahrain. He brings expertise in global governance and is a former President of the Student Council.

Leena Rahman

Head of Logistics

Leena ensures seamless conference operations. She managed logistics for 400+ delegates at NewMUN 2025 and is known for her organizational prowess.

Khalid Al-Faisal

Head of Media

Khalid leads the media team covering the conference. An aspiring filmmaker, he created the official NewMUN 2025 highlight video.

Fatima Mirza

Head of Outreach

Fatima manages school relations and delegate recruitment. She has brought in 15+ schools to participate in NewMUN 2026.

Ali Al-Amin

Head of Design

Ali is the creative force behind NewMUN's visual identity. He designed the 2026 logo, banners, and all conference materials.

Nora Al-Khalifa

Head of Finance

Nora manages the conference budget and sponsorship relations. She secured partnerships with 5 major sponsors for NewMUN 2026.
